# Supervisor Log

Track candidate supervisors and every interaction, so nothing has to be
remembered from memory. Add a new `##` entry per supervisor; add a new
bullet under "Contact Log" per interaction (in-person, email, etc.).

## Strategy

- Select a supervisor once the research direction starts to crystallize
  from `00-admin/areas-index.md` — but leave enough runway to approach
  faculty before deadlines.
- Read a candidate's 2–3 most recent publications before first contact.
- Prepare a one-page concept note before reaching out (see
  `06-thesis/` once a topic is selected).
- Prefer methodological fit and availability over prestige alone.
