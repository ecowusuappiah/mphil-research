# Workie & Azene (2021) — Bayesian Zero-Inflated Regression (Under-Five Mortality)

## Summary
Not an insurance paper — applies Bayesian zero-inflated / hurdle
negative-binomial models to under-five child mortality counts using
Ethiopian Demographic and Health Survey data.

## Why it matters to this area
This is the key "adjacent field" evidence: it shows zero-inflated/
hurdle modelling is mature and actively used in African
demographic/public-health data, but a broader FastTrack literature
search turned up almost nothing applying this same maturity to
insurance claims specifically in a developing/frontier-market
setting. That gap between adjacent-field maturity and this area's
near-silence is the strongest novelty signal found so far.

## Limitations / gap it leaves open
Confirms the *methodology* transfers to African data readily. Does
not itself say anything about insurance claims — the transfer to a
claims-generating process (structurally different from mortality
counts) is the actual open question.
