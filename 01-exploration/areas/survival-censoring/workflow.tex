\subsection*{Core Question}
How well do standard survival models (Cox PH, AFT) perform when censoring
is informative or interval-based rather than the textbook right-censored
case, and can modern statistical-learning estimators improve on them?

\subsection*{Why This Area}

priority area on the initial landscape scan: very high novelty,
feasibility, and publication potential, with a clear line to real
health/insurance data.
